Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains the conditions and procedures related to refunds for digital products, applications and educational services provided by Digital Mind Gbb.

1. Overview

Digital Mind Gbb de Freitas Servicos (“Digital Mind Gbb”, “Company”, “we”, “our” or “us”) strives to provide high-quality digital products and educational experiences.

We understand that certain situations may require a refund request. This policy outlines the terms applicable to eligible purchases.

2. 7-Day Refund Window

Eligible purchases may qualify for a refund request within seven (7) calendar days from the original purchase date.

Refund requests submitted after the 7-day period may not be eligible for approval.

3. Eligible Products & Services

Refund eligibility may apply to:

  • Digital educational products and online learning materials.
  • Subscription-based digital services where applicable.
  • Applications or digital products purchased directly through our platform.

Certain promotional offers, custom services or third-party transactions may have separate conditions or limitations.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may be denied in situations including, but not limited to:

  • Requests submitted outside the 7-day refund period.
  • Evidence of misuse, abuse or fraudulent activity.
  • Violation of our Terms of Use or platform policies.
  • Chargebacks initiated before contacting our support team.

5. Refund Process

To request a refund, users should contact our support team with relevant purchase information and details regarding the request.

Approved refunds are typically processed through the original payment method used during checkout.

Processing times may vary depending on financial institutions, payment processors or regional banking systems.

6. Subscription Cancellations

Users may cancel recurring subscriptions at any time before the next billing cycle to avoid future charges.

Cancellation of a subscription does not automatically guarantee a refund for previous payments already processed.

7. Fraud Prevention

To maintain platform integrity and security, Digital Mind Gbb reserves the right to review refund requests for abuse, suspicious activity or policy violations.

Excessive refund requests or fraudulent behavior may result in account restrictions or permanent access limitations.

8. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify or update this Refund Policy at any time to reflect operational, legal or regulatory changes.

Continued use of our services after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
